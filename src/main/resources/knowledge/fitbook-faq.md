# FitBook Studio: Policies and Frequently Asked Questions

This file is the knowledge source for the FitBook assistant. The content is synthetic: it was
written for the CMPE 172 term project and does not describe a real business. Each "## " section
is stored as one chunk in the vector store, so keep every section about a single topic.

## Opening hours
FitBook Studio is open Monday to Friday from 6:00 AM to 9:00 PM, Saturday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM,
and Sunday from 8:00 AM to 2:00 PM. The front desk is staffed during all opening hours. The studio
is closed on New Year's Day, Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day. On other public holidays it runs
the Sunday schedule.

## Location, parking and contact
The studio is at 120 Paseo Court, San Jose, California, on the second floor above the bike shop.
Free parking is available in the lot behind the building for up to two hours, and there is a bike
rack by the entrance. The front desk can be reached at 408-555-0172 or hello@fitbook.example.

## Services and prices
FitBook offers four services. 1:1 Personal Training is a 60 minute one-on-one strength and
conditioning session and costs $75. Group HIIT Class is a 45 minute high-intensity interval class
in a small group and costs $25. Vinyasa Yoga is a 60 minute flow-based yoga class for all levels
and costs $30. Mobility & Recovery is a 30 minute guided stretching and mobility session and costs
$20. Prices are per session and per person.

## Trainers
Maya Chen is the strength and conditioning coach. She is a certified strength coach with eight
years of experience and leads 1:1 Personal Training and the Group HIIT Class. Diego Alvarez is the
yoga and mobility instructor. He is an RYT-500 certified yoga teacher and leads Vinyasa Yoga and
Mobility & Recovery sessions.

## How booking works
Members book online, either from the Browse slots page or by asking the FitBook assistant. Each
time slot can be booked by one member only, so a slot disappears from the list as soon as someone
books it. Slots can be booked until their start time, and trainers usually publish their slots
about one week ahead. A booking is confirmed immediately and a confirmation message is sent to the
member's email address. There is no waitlist; if a slot is taken, pick another open slot.

## Cancellation policy
A member can cancel a booking free of charge up to 12 hours before the session starts. A late
cancellation, made less than 12 hours before the start, is charged 50 percent of the session
price. Cancelling is done from the My appointments page or by asking the assistant, and the slot
becomes available to other members straight away. Sessions cannot be transferred to another
person; cancel and let the other person book instead.

## Missed sessions and late arrival
A member who does not show up without cancelling is charged the full session price. Please arrive
10 minutes before the start time. Members who arrive more than 10 minutes late to a Group HIIT
Class or a Vinyasa Yoga class may not be able to join, for safety reasons, and the session then
counts as missed. A 1:1 Personal Training session that starts late still ends at its scheduled
time.

## What to bring
Bring a water bottle, a small towel and clean indoor training shoes. For Vinyasa Yoga and
Mobility & Recovery no shoes are needed; the studio provides yoga mats, blocks and straps free of
charge, and members are welcome to bring their own mat. For 1:1 Personal Training and Group HIIT
Class wear comfortable training clothes. Lockers are free to use, but bring your own padlock.
Showers and changing rooms are available.

## First visit and health information
New members should arrive 15 minutes early for their first session to fill in a short health
questionnaire. Tell the trainer before the session about any injury, medical condition or
pregnancy, so the exercises can be adapted. Members under 16 must be accompanied by a parent or
guardian. Anyone who feels unwell or has a fever should cancel and stay home.

## Payment and refunds
Sessions are paid at the front desk by card or contactless payment before the session starts; cash
is not accepted. FitBook does not sell memberships or packages at the moment, every session is
paid individually. If the studio or a trainer has to cancel a session, the member is not charged,
and any amount already paid is refunded in full within five business days.

## Reminders and notifications
FitBook sends a confirmation message when a booking is made and when it is cancelled. A reminder
message is sent about 24 hours before each session, with the time, the trainer and a short tip on
what to bring. Messages go to the email address on the member's account.

## Studio rules
Wipe down equipment after use and return weights to the racks. Phone calls are not allowed on the
training floor, and phones should be silent during classes. Outdoor shoes are not allowed on the
training floor or in the yoga room. Food is not allowed in the training areas; water in a closed
bottle is fine. Items left behind are kept at the front desk for 30 days.
